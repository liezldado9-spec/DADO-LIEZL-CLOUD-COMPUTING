# Reflection

Completing the Multi-Cloud Explorer mission helped me understand cloud computing better than simply reading about it. The cloud platform that impressed me the most was **Google Cloud Platform (GCP)**. I was interested in its strong support for artificial intelligence, machine learning, data analytics, and Kubernetes. I also learned that AWS and Azure have their own strengths, so choosing a cloud provider depends on what an organization actually needs.

AWS, Azure, and GCP have many similarities. All three provide cloud-based computing, storage, networking, databases, security, and other services. They also allow organizations to use virtual machines and scale their resources depending on their workload. Through the activity, I noticed that although the names of their services are different, many of them perform similar functions. For example, Amazon EC2, Azure Virtual Machines, and Google Compute Engine can all be used to host virtual machines.

Business requirements have an important role in selecting a cloud platform. An organization should consider its budget, existing technologies, workload, security needs, scalability, and future plans. For example, a company that already uses Microsoft products may benefit more from Azure, while a company focused on AI and machine learning may prefer GCP. AWS can also be a good choice for organizations that need a wide range of cloud services.

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me that choosing a cloud provider is not simply about selecting the most popular platform. I need to understand the client's goals and requirements first, then recommend the platform and services that best fit those needs.

Finally, this mission improved my GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio by adding organized research, comparisons, recommendations, Linux investigation results, screenshots, and reflections. It also gave me practical experience documenting my work using Markdown and GitHub. Overall, the mission helped me become more confident in understanding and explaining cloud computing concepts.
